Why Choose PDF Over Images?

Images (like JPG, PNG, or JPEG) often take up too much storage space, and their quality or formatting can shift when opened on different devices. On the other hand, PDF (Portable Document Format) locks the layout, ensuring that your document looks clean, sharp, and professional everywhere.
