Privacy Policy

At Pak PDF Studio, accessible from our website, one of our main priorities is the privacy of our visitors. This Privacy Policy document contains types of information that is collected and recorded by Pak PDF Studio and how we use it.

1. File Security & Client-Side Processing

All image and text-to-PDF conversions happen locally inside your web browser using client-side processing. Your files, notes, and personal data are never uploaded to our servers, ensuring 100% privacy and security.

2. Log Files

Like many standard websites, we use log files. These files log visitors when they visit websites. The information collected includes internet protocol (IP) addresses, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date and time stamp.

3. Consent

By using our website, you hereby consent to our Privacy Policy and agree to its terms.
